Nonmanufacturer rule, handled

PSG qualifies as a small business nonmanufacturer under 13 CFR 121.406(b): under 500 employees, supplying end items through its distributor network with country-of-origin verified before quoting.

Simplified acquisition, simplified

For small business set-asides between the micro-purchase and simplified acquisition thresholds, 13 CFR 121.406(c) waives the nonmanufacturer limitations entirely. That is PSG's home lane: FAR Part 13 quotes, returned complete.
